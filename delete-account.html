Delete your BIOSYNC account

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how to delete your BIOSYNC account and the data held with it. BIOSYNC is a health and wellness app; the account holds the health records you logged yourself, so deleting it removes them.

1. Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the quickest route and needs nobody's help:

  1. Open BIOSYNC and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab, and scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The deletion runs straight away. You are signed out, and your login stops working.

2. Or ask us to delete it

Email biosync.privacy@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm once it is done. We act on these within 30 days and normally much sooner.

Email us from the account's own address where you can, because that is how we know the request is yours. If you can't, say so and we will verify another way rather than delete the wrong person's records.

3. What is deleted

Everything tied to your account, which for most people is all of it:

  • Your daily check-ins, metrics, readiness scores, goals and journal entries.
  • Your nutrition and supplement logs.
  • Your progress photos, including the image files.
  • Your training sessions, plans, class bookings and gym check-ins.
  • Your treatment requests and the decisions recorded on them.
  • Your consent settings and the links to anyone on your care team.
  • Your login, including your email address and password.

4. What is kept, and for how long

One thing outlives the deletion, and we would rather say so plainly than let you find out later:

  • Encrypted backups — up to 90 days. The database is backed up every night so that a fault cannot lose everyone's records. Each backup is destroyed 90 days after it is taken, so a deleted account can still sit inside an encrypted backup until then. Those backups are only ever used to recover from a failure, never to bring back an account you asked us to delete.

Nothing else is held back. The record of who on your care team opened your data is deleted with the account, and so are the notes and plans a PT wrote about you.

5. Things worth knowing first

  • It cannot be undone. There is no restore, and we will not recover it from a backup.
  • Take a copy first if you want one. You → Download your data hands you your records before you delete, or email us for a portable file.
  • Treatment requests go too. If a request is with the Biomeds doctor and you still want it reviewed, deal with that before deleting.
  • Deleting the app is not deleting the account. Removing BIOSYNC from your phone leaves the account and its records in place — use one of the two routes above.

6. Questions or complaints

Email biosync.privacy@gmail.com. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why. If you think we have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
