BIOSYNC Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

1. Who we are

BIOSYNC is a health and wellness platform built by Kaze. The app helps users track daily check-ins, metrics, nutrition, supplements, and readiness scores. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the data needed to operate the app:

  • Account data: email address, full name, and password (stored by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Health data: daily check-ins (sleep, energy, mood), point metrics (heart rate, weight, HRV, glucose), nutrition logs, supplement logs, readiness scores, goals, and journal entries.
  • Staff data (if applicable): if a trainer or practitioner uses the staff features, they can view limited client data only where the client has given explicit consent.
  • Usage data: crash reports (via Sentry) and basic performance metrics to keep the app stable.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the core app features (check-ins, dashboards, trends, coach, nutrition).
  • To generate readiness scores and insights from your own data.
  • To keep your account secure and authenticate you.
  • To fix crashes and improve stability.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use your health data for advertising.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) in the EU. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth and are never visible to us. All API communication uses HTTPS. The mobile app authenticates with short-lived JWTs.

5. Third-party services

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and storage. Privacy Policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Render: API hosting. Privacy Policy: render.com/privacy
  • Groq: AI coach, nutrition estimates, and supplement suggestions. Privacy Policy: groq.com/privacy
  • Sentry: crash reporting. Privacy Policy: sentry.io/privacy

6. Your rights

  • Access: you can view all your data inside the app.
  • Correction: you can edit or delete individual logs, metrics, meals, and supplements.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account at any time from Settings. This permanently removes all your data from our database and your auth account from Supabase.
  • Export: contact us if you want a copy of your data in a portable format.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

BIOSYNC is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app and update this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at: biosync.privacy@gmail.com
